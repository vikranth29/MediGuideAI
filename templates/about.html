About MediGuide AI

Empowering Patients with
Intelligent Health Insights

MediGuide AI is a final-year project that demonstrates how modern web architectures and artificial intelligence can assist individuals in understanding health symptoms.

Our Mission

Making Healthcare Accessible for Everyone

We believe that health information should be immediate, clear, and accessible to all. MediGuide AI bridges the gap between technology and medical awareness by combining structured medical knowledge with a user-friendly AI interface.
