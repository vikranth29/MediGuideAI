Paracetamol

Pain Reliever
Uses

Used to reduce fever and relieve mild to moderate pain.

Dosage

Adults: 500mg to 1000mg every 4–6 hours. Maximum 4000mg per day.

Precautions
  • Do not exceed the recommended dose.
  • Avoid alcohol while taking this medicine.
  • Consult a doctor if symptoms continue.
